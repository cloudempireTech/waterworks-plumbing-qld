Powerful Drain Clearing

5,000 PSI Water Jetting Beats Traditional Snakes

Old-fashioned mechanical drain cables ("electric eels") merely punch a small hole through the center of a root mass, leaving behind thick root collars that snag toilet paper and re-block within weeks.

Our trailer-mounted commercial hydro-jetters deliver specialized spinning stainless nozzles at 5,000 PSI. The rear-facing propulsion jets power down the pipe while rotating front blades scour the full 360-degree diameter of the pipe wall, completely flushing roots, cooking fat, and silt out to the council main.

Signs of an Underground Drainage Blockage:

  • Gurgling sounds from floor waste gullies when flushing toilets
  • Water rising dangerously in the toilet bowl before slowly draining
  • Foul sewer odour rising from laundry floor grates or showers
  • Overflow Relief Gully (ORG) outside spilling wastewater into the garden

High-Definition CCTV Color Camera Inspections

Don't dig up your driveway, lawn, or tiled patio on guesswork. With our self-leveling fiber optic drain camera systems, we view live color video footage inside underground pipes.

Our built-in 512Hz sonde transmitters allow our plumbers to trace the camera head from above ground, pinpointing the exact depth and surface position of cracked earthenware, collapsed PVC, or heavy root masses to within a few centimeters.
