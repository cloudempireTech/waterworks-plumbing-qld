Gas Fitting Specialists

Safe, Certified Gas Work Done Right

Working with combustible gas requires meticulous technical care and strict adherence to AS/NZS 5601 standards. At Waterworks Plumbing Qld, our licensed gasfitters install, test, and commission residential and commercial gas systems.

Whether upgrading to an instantaneous gas hot water system, connecting a premium chef's cooktop, or running an outdoor BBQ bayonet point, we test every joint with precision digital manometers and issue official Queensland Gas Compliance Certificates.

  • Gas Compliance Plates & Certificates: Mandatory legal documentation for insurance and warranty validation.
  • LPG & Natural Gas Conversions: Regulators, pigtail lines, and burner jet sizing for all appliances.
  • Emergency Leak Detection: Sensitive gas sniffers to isolate tiny leaks inside cavities.
